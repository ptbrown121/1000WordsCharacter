📖 How to Use This Sheet

🧑‍🎤 Getting Started

Click your character's name at the top left to rename them. Set your starting XP in the header (default is 75 for a new character).

📊 Stats & Attributes

Each attribute (BODY, POWER, SOUL, etc.) accepts a comma-separated list of dice. For example, type d6, d8 to assign two dice to that stat.

  • Auto-Calculate XP — Totals the XP cost of all your attributes and tiles.
  • Auto-Calculate Vitals — Sets your HP, EN, RX, and SH max values based on your attributes and tile colors, then fills your current values to match.

🧩 Mosaic (Tiles)

Tiles represent your character's skills, gear, traits, and story elements. Click + Add Tile to create one, or + Add Spell to use the guided spell builder.

  • Colors determine which dice pool a tile contributes to and which vitals it boosts.
  • Tags add mechanical effects (Chain, Hitch, Expert, etc.).
  • ✏️ Edit on any card lets you update it later to spend more XP.
  • 🔥 Burn marks a tile as spent for the session. Use 🏕️ Rest to un-burn all tiles.

Use the search bar to filter tiles by name, color, type, or tags.

🎲 The Call (Dice Pool)

When the GM calls for a roll, select 1–2 Call Colors. Your matching stat dice are automatically added to the pool. Then:

  1. Click a tile in your Mosaic to set it as the Call Tile (first click) or Burn Tile (second click).
  2. Add any extra dice from spending Energy or Shadow in the Extra Dice field.
  3. Choose Virtual Roll to let the app roll for you, or Manual Input to enter physical dice results.

The Auto-Filter checkbox (on by default) will hide tiles that don't match the called colors.

❤️ Vitals & Bonuses

HP, EN, RX, and SH track your character's resources. The current value (left) is editable — adjust it as you take damage or spend energy.

Click the ⚙️ gear icon next to any vital's max to set Permanent or Temporary bonuses. Temp bonuses show a golden badge as a reminder.

💾 Saving & Sharing

Your character is auto-saved to your browser's local storage. Use ⬇️ Export to download a JSON file you can share or back up, and ⬆️ Import to load one.

📓 Journal

Scroll to the bottom to find the Journal section. Create named entries for session notes, adventure logs, or anything else. Entries auto-save as you type and are included in exports.
